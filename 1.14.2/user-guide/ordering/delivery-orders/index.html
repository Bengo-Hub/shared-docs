    Delivery orders¶

    A delivery order is accepted and prepared like a pickup order, then a rider takes it to the customer. This page follows one from Demo City Supermarket to a customer in Kondele (KES 150 delivery), paid on delivery.

    1. Accept and prepare¶

    The order rings under Delivery in the POS Online orders queue. Accept it the same way as a pickup order.

    In a shop the accepted order becomes a pick list under To pick & pack. The counter picks the items, bags them and presses Packed & ready.

    In a restaurant the accepted order goes to the kitchen as a ticket marked Online delivery. The kitchen presses Start, then Ready when the food is packed. A kitchen that works from printed chits instead presses Ready on the queue card.

    Either way the customer is told it is ready, the card moves to Ready for the rider, and a delivery job is created for your riders.

    The kitchen ticket closes by itself when the rider collects the order. From here on a restaurant delivery works exactly like a shop's.

    2. Getting a rider¶

    The job goes to a rider in one of three ways, depending on your dispatch settings:

    • Automatically to the nearest available rider on shift.
    • By a dispatcher, from Logistics, Deliveries (or Assign rider on the POS card).
    • By a rider taking it from Available Work in the rider app (Take job). Only one rider can take a job.

    Once a rider has the job, the POS card shows their name and phone and the customer sees who is coming.

    3. The rider app¶

    Riders use the rider app on their phone (https://riderapp.codevertexafrica.com). To get jobs they switch themselves online, which also shares their location while they work.

    Available Work on the rider app home lists jobs waiting for a rider, with the outlet, the area and the fee. Take job gives it to that rider.

    After taking a job the rider works through it one button at a time (on the way to the outlet, at the outlet, picked up, on the way, at the customer), with the route on the map:

    1. At the counter, checks the bag against the order on screen and marks it Picked up. The customer's page changes to On the way and shows the rider on the map. Any kitchen tickets still open for the order close.
    2. At the door, asks for the customer's 6-digit delivery code, enters it and writes who received the order. The code is checked by the system, not the phone; after five wrong codes the job stops taking codes and the dispatcher is alerted.
    3. For pay on delivery, records how the customer paid: cash, or M-Pesa with the code. The app says Collect payment at the door with the amount.
    4. Presses Confirm & Complete. The order is delivered, the customer is told, and the stock is taken off.

    If the customer cannot find the code, the rider presses Customer can't find the code? Ask the dispatcher. The rider cannot finish the delivery without the code; see The customer has no code.

    If the customer is not reachable or refuses the order, the rider reports a failed delivery with a reason instead (see When things go wrong).

    4. Rider cash¶

    Cash a rider collects belongs to the business until they hand it in. Riders see what they hold as Cash to hand in on their app. In Logistics, Rider cash, the dispatcher or manager counts the cash, enters the amount received with Record hand-in, and any shortfall stays on the record for a manager to follow up.

    Delivering with your own staff¶

    Staff who deliver for you are added as riders in Logistics, Riders and use the rider app like anyone else. Every delivery ends with the customer's delivery code in the rider app, so the POS has no button to mark a delivery as delivered. Once the rider has delivered, the order closes by itself; a card still on the queue shows Close order.

    What the customer sees¶

    On WhatsApp the customer gets the rider's name and phone when the order leaves, and a thank-you with Leave Feedback and Review us on Google once it is delivered.
