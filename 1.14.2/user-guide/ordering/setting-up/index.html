    Setting up online ordering¶

    Before your store takes its first order, five things need to be in place: the menu, where your outlets are, how customers can pay, where you deliver, and who delivers. Each lives in the app that owns it, and the online store reads it from there, so you set it once and the POS, the store and the riders all see the same thing.

    What Where you set it
    Menu, photos, prices, stock Inventory, Catalogue
    Outlet address and map pin Accounts, My Organisation, Branches
    Payment methods (M-Pesa, PayHero, card, cash on delivery) Treasury, Settings, Payments
    Your own M-Pesa Till or Paybill for "pay and enter the code" POS, Settings, Payments, for each outlet
    Customer messages and sender name Notifications, Settings
    Delivery areas and fees Logistics, Delivery areas
    Riders and their vehicles Logistics, Riders
    Order acceptance, store fees, payment retry time Online store dashboard, Settings
    App name and icon (for example "Urban Eats") Accounts, My Organisation, Branding, App Names and Icons

    The store's own settings¶

    Open the online store, sign in as an admin, and choose Settings in the dashboard menu.

    Order acceptance¶

    • Manual (recommended). Every new order rings in the POS Online orders queue and waits until someone accepts it. The kitchen only gets it after that, so nothing is cooked for an order you cannot fulfil.
    • Automatic. An order goes straight to the kitchen once it is paid, or as soon as it is placed when the customer pays on delivery or at the counter. Staff can still reject it.

    Fees and service configuration¶

    Below acceptance you set the store's own fees (packaging and service fees). The delivery fee is not set here: it comes from the delivery area the customer's location falls in.

    Service configuration lists the store's remaining settings. The one most businesses change is orders.payment_retry_window_minutes: how long an order that was not paid online keeps its items reserved and lets the customer try paying again (30 minutes unless you change it, between 5 minutes and a day).

    Everything else¶

    The Managed in other apps card opens each of the other apps on the right page for your business.

    Outlet locations¶

    Delivery fees and rider directions are worked out from each outlet's map pin. Open Accounts, My Organisation, Branches, edit the outlet, and place the pin on its entrance. An outlet with no pin can still take pickup orders but cannot deliver.

    Delivery areas¶

    In Logistics, Delivery areas you draw where you deliver and what it costs. The demo business delivers from Kisumu CBD like this:

    Area Fee
    Kisumu CBD (2 km around Kisumu Central) Free
    Milimani, Tom Mboya, Polyview KES 100
    Kondele, Manyatta, Migosi, Lolwe, Nyalenda KES 150
    Nyamasaria, Dunga KES 200
    Mamboleo, Riat, Kisumu Airport KES 250
    Otonglo KES 300
    Kisian KES 350
    Maseno, Ahero KES 600

    A few things worth knowing:

    • An area can serve chosen outlets only. The demo's areas serve the hotel and the supermarket; its kiosk is pickup only.
    • Smaller areas win where they overlap. Give the big free circle a lower priority (10) than the estates inside it (20) so an estate's own fee applies there.
    • Places between areas are priced per kilometre along the road (the demo charges KES 50 a km, at least KES 100) when they lie on the way to one of your areas. A place far off every route is refused, and the customer is told the nearest area you do deliver to.
    • A pin at the outlet itself (within 200 metres) is treated as pickup: the customer is standing in your shop.
    • Test a location shows the fee and the reason for any place before a customer finds out.

    Riders¶

    In Logistics, Riders, add each rider with Add rider. A rider signs in to the rider app with their own account, so give them an email they can open.

    • Employment. A staff rider is paid by you as an employee (per diem claims go to HR); a freelance rider earns per delivery and goes through a document check before getting jobs.
    • Vehicle and insurance. Link the rider's vehicle. A motorbike or car with no insurance recorded, or with expired insurance, gets no jobs unless you change that rule in Logistics settings. Bicycles never need insurance. Riders and you are reminded before a policy expires.
    • Phone number. Customers see the rider's name and phone on the tracking page once the rider has the job. Riders keep their number up to date on their profile in the rider app.

    How jobs reach riders¶

    Logistics, Settings, Dispatch and delivery has three switches:

    Setting On Off
    Auto-assign new deliveries (Growth plans and up) The nearest available rider on shift gets the job Jobs wait for a dispatcher
    Riders can take open jobs Riders on shift see unassigned jobs under Available Work and take one; only one rider gets each job Riders only get jobs given to them
    Require proof of delivery Riders finish a job with the customer's code or a photo Riders can mark a job delivered without proof

    A rider only gets jobs while their app shows them as online and their phone is sharing its location.
