## Recommended Package: Codevertex Afya Clinic + Inpatient Add-on

Your patient volume and the four modules you listed map closely to our **Afya Clinic** tier — the package built for dispensaries, health centres, and small clinics running consultation, pharmacy, and billing from a single outlet. Because you also see inpatients, we've added the **Inpatient add-on module** so short-stay admissions are tracked without paying for the full multi-department Facility tier your current volume doesn't yet need. You can upgrade to Afya Facility at any time as your patient numbers grow — your data moves with you, nothing is re-entered.

### What's included

**Reception & queuing** — patient registration, check-in, appointment booking, single patient record (EMR) shared by every department.

**Consultation module** — doctor queue, structured examination notes, diagnosis capture, referral to lab or pharmacy.

**Laboratory (referred-out)** — test requests, result capture, and delivery back to the consulting doctor. *(In-house lab with a full test catalogue and sample tracking is available on the Afya Facility tier.)*

**Pharmacy** — prescription dispensing, over-the-counter sale, stock-linked drug dispensing with batch/expiry tracking and drug-interaction checks.

**Billing & Finance** — cashier till, patient invoicing, M-Pesa and card payment collection. KRA eTIMS/ETR-compliant invoicing is available as an **opt-in** for services or facilities that require a fiscal invoice — it is not switched on for every billable service by default.

**Inpatient add-on** — basic ward/bed tracking for short-stay observation and admission-to-discharge records.

**Built in on every tier**: SHA/SHIF and NHIF claims & eligibility verification (aligned to the 2026 Taifa Care HMIS transition), cloud hosting with automatic backups, Kenya Data Protection Act-aligned audit trails and consent capture, and free updates with local support.
