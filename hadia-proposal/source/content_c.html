Process workflows

Each workflow defines its trigger, steps, automation and exception handling. Steps marked auto run without user or staff action.

Item listing

  1. The owner pastes a product link or selects manual entry.
  2. For links, the server retrieves public page metadata under strict controls: public addresses only, 3-second timeout, 1 MB limit, no cookies.
  3. Title, image and price are prefilled for owner confirmation. Where no data is found, the manual form is shown.
  4. Photos are converted to WebP at 640 and 1280 pixels, stored in object storage and served through the CDN. The source store is credited.
  5. The owner sets the item mode: reserve, fund, or both.

Sharing

  1. Each registry receives a short link; premium registries may set a custom link.
  2. A 1200 by 630 pixel preview image under 300 KB is generated for WhatsApp and social platforms. auto
  3. Sharing options: WhatsApp, SMS, copy link and QR code.

Item reservation

  1. The guest selects Reserve and enters a name and, optionally, a mobile number.
  2. The reservation is recorded in a single conditional update. Concurrent requests for the last unit result in one reservation; other guests are offered a contribution instead.
  3. The guest receives a cancellation link. The item is shown as reserved to all guests.
  4. After 14 days without purchase confirmation, reminders are sent and the owner may release the item. auto

Cash contribution

  1. The guest selects an item or fund and enters an amount and mobile number. Limits are validated and the total, including any processing fee, is shown.
  2. hadia-ui submits the contribution to treasury with an idempotency key.
  3. Treasury initiates a PayHero collection into the Hadia wallet. The guest authorises the STK push.
  4. On callback, treasury verifies the transaction with PayHero, credits the registry once and posts the ledger entry. auto
  5. The checkout page confirms payment. Treasury publishes the credit event.
  6. hadia-api updates item progress and the gift log and triggers the guest receipt and owner notification. auto

Exceptions: failed or cancelled prompts leave the contribution pending and the paybill option is offered. Repeated failures to one number (five in 24 hours) suspend further prompts to that number. Missing callbacks are resolved by status enquiry.

Registry management

  1. The dashboard shows amounts received, paid out, pending and the next payout.
  2. Owners may change the target, threshold (within Hadia's limits) and event date. The payout number changes only after verification of the new number and never during an active payout.
  3. Contributions above an item's price remain in the registry balance and are paid out with it.
  4. After the hold period the registry closes to new contributions. Owners may reopen it.

Automated payout

Registries meeting a payout rule are paid every 5 minutes to the verified number, net of commission, with approval required only at or above Hadia's limit. The owner receives an SMS showing gross amount, commission and net amount. auto Full process in Funds management and payouts.

Commission and sweep

Commission is deducted at payout, recorded as Hadia income and transferred daily to Hadia's business account above the sweep minimum. auto eTIMS invoices for Hadia's fees are issued through treasury's KRA integration.

Premium upgrade

  1. The owner selects Upgrade and is shown the features and price.
  2. hadia-api creates a payment intent in treasury and opens the shared payment modal.
  3. On confirmation, treasury records the sale as Hadia revenue and hadia-api enables the custom link, printable list and instant payouts. auto
  4. A receipt is issued. Refunds follow the refund policy.

Cancellation and refunds

  1. The owner requests cancellation, or Hadia cancels following a fraud or policy finding.
  2. The refund is authorised under the approval policy, bound to the registry and its balance.
  3. The registry is closed to new contributions. Each contribution is refunded to the paying number under its own reference. auto
  4. Failed refunds return to the registry balance for retry. Repeated cancellation requests never duplicate a refund. Contributions without a number are listed for manual settlement.

Exceptions and reconciliation

  1. Reconciliation runs hourly. auto
  2. The exception queue lists failed payouts, name mismatches, manual refunds, reconciliation variances, reported registries and pending approvals.
  3. Each entry records the facts, recommended action and resolver. Finance receives a daily summary.

Thank-you tracking

  1. All contributions and reservations appear in the gift log with guest messages.
  2. The owner sends a prepared WhatsApp thank-you and marks the guest as thanked.
  3. A reminder listing outstanding thank-yous is sent one week after the event. Owners may disable it.

Data subject requests

  1. An owner or guest submits a request for access, correction or deletion.
  2. Identity is confirmed by one-time password and the statutory deadline is recorded.
  3. Access requests produce a machine-readable export.
  4. Deletion erases or anonymises personal data. Financial records required by law are retained for the statutory period under a reference, and the requester is informed.

Data design

hadia-api owns the registry domain and references other services by ID: the owner's user ID in auth-api and the escrow account and payment intent IDs in treasury.

Codevertex access to Hadia data

Codevertex staff do not access Hadia's personal or financial records in normal operation. Access occurs only to resolve a support request raised by Hadia, to investigate an incident, or to perform a restore, and is limited to the records concerned. Each such access is logged and reported to Hadia on request. These obligations are set out in the data processing agreement, and every engineer with production access is bound by a confidentiality undertaking.

Penetration testing

An internal security review against the OWASP Application Security Verification Standard is included in week 11. An independent penetration test by an accredited third party is recommended before launch and annually thereafter. It is commissioned at Hadia's cost at the tester's rate; Codevertex supports the test and remediates all high and critical findings at no charge before launch.
