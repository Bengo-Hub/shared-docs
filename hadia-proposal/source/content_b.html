Architecture principles

  • Single data owner. hadia-api references treasury records by ID. Balances are read from treasury.
  • Single payment path. Only treasury-api communicates with PayHero.
  • Event driven. Treasury publishes escrow events through a transactional outbox to NATS. hadia-api consumes them idempotently.
  • Replica safe. Scheduled jobs run once per period across all replicas using shared leases.
  • Standard stack. Go with Ent and Atlas migrations, Next.js, Helm and ArgoCD.

Funds management and payouts

Custody

Hadia operates as a tenant on the Codevertex platform with its own PayHero Team account, registered in Hadia's name and verified to PayHero KYC tier 3. The Team wallet is isolated and used only by Hadia. All contributions are received into this wallet. Treasury maintains a separate sub-ledger for each registry, so the wallet balance is fully allocated between registries and Hadia's earned commission at all times.

Codevertex does not receive or hold Hadia funds or customer funds.

Registry escrow account

Each published registry has an escrow account in treasury. The account holds the registry title, the owner as beneficiary with a verified M-Pesa number, the target, payout rules and gift items. The registry ID is used as the external reference, which prevents duplicate accounts on retry.

Payout process

Each payout attempt is registered under a unique reference before PayHero is called. Only one payout may be active per registry. A failed payout returns the funds to the registry balance. Payouts without a callback after 15 minutes are confirmed by status enquiry. All results are verified with PayHero before posting.

Commission

Commission is deducted from the gross payout and the net amount is paid to the owner. Commission is recorded in income account 4800 and retained in the Team wallet until swept.

  1. The sweep runs daily, or after each payout if configured.
  2. Available commission equals the wallet balance less registry balances, payouts in progress and the operating reserve.
  3. Amounts above the sweep minimum are paid to Hadia's verified business channel. Changing the destination requires MFA and re-verification.
  4. The transfer is posted from the PayHero wallet account to Hadia's bank or paybill account. The sweep is suspended if reconciliation reports a shortfall.

Manual withdrawal of commission remains available as an alternative.

Reconciliation

Treasury compares the total of registry balances and payouts in progress with the PayHero wallet balance every hour. Any shortfall is raised in the exception queue and suspends the commission sweep until resolved. Each registry statement is available in PDF, CSV and Excel.

Commission basis and configuration

QuestionPosition
Basis of calculationCommission is calculated on gross contributions. Each registry is credited with the full amount the guest paid; PayHero's collection charge is paid separately from Hadia's PayHero service wallet and is never deducted from the registry.
Worked exampleA guest gives KES 2,000. The registry is credited KES 2,000. At payout, 4% commission (KES 80) is deducted and the owner receives KES 1,920. PayHero's collection charge for the KES 2,000 payment is debited from Hadia's service wallet.
Changing the rateHadia finance changes the commission rule (percentage, fixed amount, minimum and maximum fee) at any time from the administration console. Changes apply to payouts made after the change and are recorded in the audit log.
Per-registry ratesA different rate, including 0%, can be set on individual registries, for example for partner planners or VIP clients.
Promotional periodsHadia can schedule a promotion with a start date, end date and rate, including 0%. Registries created within the window keep the promotional rate for their lifetime.

Payout controls

ControlHow it works
Freeze a payoutHadia can freeze any registry. Automatic payouts stop and the balance is held until the freeze is lifted. A platform-wide freeze switches every payout to manual approval.

Reversals, fraud and chargebacks

EventHandlingLoss allocation
Payment reversed before payoutThe reversal is debited from the registry balance and the owner is notified.No loss

Card chargebackCard contributions become eligible for payout only after a 14-day hold. A chargeback is debited from the registry balance first, then from Hadia's commission reserve, then recovered from the owner. Hadia responds to the dispute with the contribution evidence held in the ledger.Hadia, pending recovery
Fraudulent or unauthorised paymentFrozen on detection; refunded to the payer where confirmed. Monitoring flags unusual patterns such as many large gifts from few numbers.Hadia as operator, except where caused by a Codevertex software defect

M-Pesa STK payments are authorised by the payer's PIN, so unauthorised M-Pesa contributions are rare. Card chargeback rights last up to 120 days under card scheme rules, which is why card-funded gifts carry the 14-day hold and a commission reserve.
