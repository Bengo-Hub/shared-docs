Design rules we hold to

  • One owner per piece of data. hadia-api stores treasury's pot ID, never a copy of the balance as truth. Its gift log is a projection rebuilt from treasury events.
  • Money moves in one place. Only treasury-api talks to PayHero. hadia-api asks and listens.
  • Events first. Treasury publishes escrow events through its transactional outbox to NATS. hadia-api consumes them idempotently, keyed on the event ID and the payment intent ID.
  • Safe with many replicas. Scheduled jobs use the shared cache leases so each runs once per period across the fleet. Workers claim rows with conditional updates.
  • Same stack as the fleet. Go with Ent and Atlas migrations, Next.js with the shared UI library, Helm and ArgoCD for deploys. Any Codevertex engineer can support Hadia.

Money, escrow and automated payouts

This chapter explains where gift money sits, how it is accounted for, and how payouts and Hadia's fee happen without anyone pressing a button. It is the part of the system that most affects trust, so it is described in more detail than the rest.

Where the money is

Hadia is a tenant on the Codevertex platform. Hadia gets its own PayHero Team account, in Hadia's name and verified to PayHero's KYC tier 3 with Hadia's company documents. The Team has an isolated wallet that no other tenant and not the platform itself can use; treasury never falls back to another account for a Team tenant. Every guest contribution lands in that wallet. Treasury keeps one sub-ledger (a pot wallet) per registry, so the wallet's balance is always split, to the shilling, between the registries that own it and Hadia's commission.

Codevertex never receives or holds Hadia's or its users' money. PayHero holds it; treasury records it and instructs payouts. Codevertex is paid only for software, through Hadia's subscription.

The pot behind every registry

When an owner's registry goes live, hadia-api asks treasury to open an escrow pot. The pot carries the registry title, the owner as beneficiary (their verified M-Pesa number), the target, the payout rules and the gift items. Pots are created with an external ID equal to the registry ID, so a retry can never open a second pot.

Payout rules Hadia controls

The client wants payouts to run by thresholds and limits with as little human action as possible. Treasury's escrow module already supports this through each pot's rules and Hadia's payout policy. Hadia sets defaults once; owners can choose within the limits Hadia allows.

Automatic payout, end to end

Every safeguard in the figure already exists in treasury today: a payout is claimed under a reference unique to the attempt (ESC-code-1, ESC-code-2 and so on) before PayHero is called, only one release can be in flight per pot, a refused or failed payout returns the money to the pot, and a payout whose callback never arrives is looked up with PayHero after 15 minutes. Callbacks are treated as hints; treasury confirms each result with PayHero before booking it.

Hadia's fee: deducted, retained, then swept

At each payout the commission is subtracted from the gross and the owner receives the net. The commission stays in Hadia's PayHero wallet and is booked as income in account 4800. A new scheduled sweep moves it out:

  1. The sweep runs daily (and can also run right after a payout, if Hadia prefers).
  2. It works out what is free to move: the PayHero wallet balance, less the total of all pot balances, less releases in flight, less a reserve Hadia keeps for PayHero costs. The hourly reconciliation already computes this figure.
  3. If the free amount is above the sweep floor, treasury pays it to Hadia's own channel through the same payout dispatcher. The destination is locked to one of Hadia's verified channels and changing it needs MFA and resets verification.
  4. The books record the transfer from the PayHero wallet account to Hadia's bank or paybill account. If the reconciliation shows a shortfall, the sweep does not run and finance is alerted.

Hadia can also choose to retain commission in the wallet and withdraw it by hand, which treasury already supports.

Reconciliation

Every hour treasury compares the sum of all pot balances plus payouts in flight with the PayHero wallet balance. The surplus is Hadia's commission and other wallet money less PayHero's costs. A shortfall is logged as an error, raised in the exception queue and blocks the commission sweep until it is explained. Each pot also has its own statement (contributions, payouts, returns) that can be downloaded as PDF, CSV or Excel.
