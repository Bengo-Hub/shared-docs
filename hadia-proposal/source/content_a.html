Executive summary

Codevertex Africa Limited proposes to design, build and launch the Hadia Gifting Registry, a mobile-first platform on which registry owners list gifts for an occasion, share a single link, and receive item reservations and M-Pesa cash contributions from guests. Cash contributions are held in escrow and paid out to owners automatically, net of Hadia's commission, under rules configured by Hadia.

The platform is built on Codevertex's production services for identity, payments, accounting, escrow, approvals and messaging. The registry service, web application and administration console are developed specifically for Hadia.

Key outcomes

  • Registries published and shared within five minutes of sign-up, with no application download for owners or guests.
  • Duplicate gifts eliminated through atomic item reservations visible to all guests in real time.
  • M-Pesa contributions credited exactly once and reconciled hourly against the PayHero wallet.
  • Payouts and commission deductions executed automatically by threshold and event date, with approval required only above Hadia's limit.
  • Commission transferred to Hadia's business account daily without manual intervention.
  • Compliance with the Data Protection Act, consumer protection, payments and accessibility requirements from launch.

Overall description

Product perspective

Hadia operates as a tenant of the Codevertex platform. Two new applications, hadia-ui and hadia-api, provide the registry product. Identity, payments, ledger, escrow, approvals and messaging are provided by existing platform services, described in Solution architecture.

Product functions

  • Registry creation and management for weddings, baby showers, housewarmings, graduations and birthdays
  • Item listing by manual entry or product link, with reserve, fund or combined modes
  • Guest reservations without duplicates, and cash contributions by M-Pesa
  • Escrow of contributions with automated payouts and commission deduction
  • Premium upgrade per registry: custom link, printable list, instant payouts
  • Administration, reporting, reconciliation and compliance management

Operating environment

  • Guests and owners: any modern mobile or desktop browser; installable as a PWA
  • Hosting: Codevertex Kubernetes platform with PostgreSQL, Redis, NATS JetStream and object storage, behind Cloudflare
  • Payments: PayHero (M-Pesa, Airtel Money, card); messaging: Africa's Talking, WhatsApp, Brevo

Constraints

  • Funds are collected, held and disbursed only through a licensed payment service provider.
  • The registry service does not move money; all payments are executed by the treasury service.
  • Retailer product data is inconsistent; manual item entry is the primary method.
  • Payment onboarding depends on Hadia's company registration and PayHero KYC.

Assumptions and dependencies

  • Hadia is a registered Kenyan company with a KRA PIN and business bank account or paybill by week 2.
  • Hadia completes PayHero KYC to tier 3 and provides requested documents within 3 working days.
  • Hadia appoints a product owner with authority to decide within 2 working days.
  • Hadia provides brand assets, approves copy and supplies consented testimonials.
  • Hadia engages a Kenyan advocate to approve the legal pack and advise on payment regulation.
  • Hadia recruits 3 to 5 pilot events for week 11 and registers the domain name.
  • Hadia subscribes to the Codevertex plan with escrow and M-Pesa features, included in running costs.
