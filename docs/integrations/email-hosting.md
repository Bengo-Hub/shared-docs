# Email Hosting

Codevertex runs its own hosted email platform (Stalwart Mail Server under the hood) — custom-domain
mailboxes, webmail, IMAP/SMTP, and calendar/contacts on the higher tiers — sold per mailbox, per
month, the same way Zoho Mail or Google Workspace license seats.

## Who this is for

Any tenant that wants professional email on their own domain (`you@yourbusiness.com`) without
running their own mail server. This is a hosting product for your team's mailboxes, not a
developer API — there's nothing to integrate against unless you're building on top of your own
mailbox via standard IMAP/SMTP.

Paying annually gets a 20% discount on all three plans. Every plan includes a custom domain,
platform-managed MFA/identity via Codevertex SSO, mail forwarding, and migration assistance from
Standard upward.

## Getting started

Email hosting is provisioned per-tenant from your organisation's own subscriptions dashboard —
add a mailbox license, verify your domain's DNS records, and your team is receiving mail on their
own address within minutes. See your tenant's **Email Hosting** page under Subscriptions for the
self-service setup flow (add a domain, assign licenses, manage mailboxes).
