# The resident portal

This page is written for owners and residents. Estate offices can share it as it is.

## Signing in

Open the link your estate sent you (or `maskaniapp.codevertexafrica.com`, then type the estate code
the office gave you) and choose **Resident portal**. Enter your phone number; you get a code on WhatsApp.
There is no password.

## What you can do

- **See your units and accounts.** Each account shows what you owe, when you last paid and how to pay
  by paybill, including the account number to type.
- **Pay.** Press **Pay now** to get an M-Pesa prompt on your phone. For more than M-Pesa allows
  (KES 250,000), pay by bank transfer or cheque and enter the reference; the office confirms it once
  the money arrives.
- **Read your statement** and download it as a PDF.
- **Query a bill** within 30 days of it if something looks wrong. The office answers within a week and
  you get the answer by email.
- **Raise a request** for a repair, with photos, and follow it until it is done. Confirm the work when
  you are happy, or reopen it within seven days.
- **Create gate passes** for guests, deliveries, contractors and domestic staff. Your visitor gets a
  code by WhatsApp.
- **Answer the gate.** When a guard rings you about a walk-in, you get a notification to let them in
  or decline. Allow notifications the first time the portal asks; on an iPhone, add the portal to the
  Home Screen first (Share, then Add to Home Screen).
- **Read notices** from the estate office.
- **Manage your household** (owners and the person renting the unit). Under **Household**, add family
  members and domestic staff, then create gate passes for your staff from Visitors. Add your cars so
  the guards recognise them. Remove anyone who leaves; their passes stop at once.
- **Look after your data.** Under the account menu, **Your data and privacy** shows what the estate
  keeps and the terms you accepted. Ask there for a copy of your data, a correction, or to have your
  details erased; the estate answers within 7 or 14 days. See
  [Privacy and personal data](privacy-and-data.md).

## Install it on your phone

The portal works like an app. On Android choose **Install** when your browser offers it; on an iPhone
use Share, then Add to Home Screen.
