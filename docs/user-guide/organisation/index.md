# Account & Organisation

Every Codevertex Africa product — Inventory, POS, Treasury, and the rest — shares one account,
one login, and one place to manage your organisation. This section covers that shared layer: the
centralized SSO portal at **accounts.codevertexafrica.com**.

- **[Signing In](signing-in.md)** — logging in, resetting a forgotten password, and the other
  sign-in options available.
- **[Managing Your Organisation](managing-your-organisation.md)** — the "My Organization" hub:
  branding, branches/outlets, your team and their access, billing, and getting support. This is
  also where a new staff member's PIN gets set up — the same PIN used to log in to Inventory, POS,
  and the other products.

This guide is written for **organisation admins**. If you're staff logging in to do your job day
to day — adding products, ringing up sales, and so on — see that product's own Service Guide
instead (for example, [Inventory](../inventory/index.md)); it links back here for anything that's
managed centrally rather than per-product.

## Before you start

You'll need an account. If your organisation is already on Codevertex Africa, ask your admin to
add you (see [Managing Your Organisation](managing-your-organisation.md#team)) — you don't sign
up separately for each product.
