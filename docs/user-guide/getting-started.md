# Getting Started

## Signing up

1. **Create your account** with your name, email, and a password.
2. **Set up your organisation.** If you're starting fresh, you'll name your business, choose your industry (retail, hospitality, pharmacy, and so on — this tailors the products to how your business actually works), and pick a size. If a colleague has already set up your organisation, you can join it instead by searching for it and requesting access.
3. **Choose a plan.** Every plan comes with a 14-day free trial, so you can try the platform with your own data before committing. See [Subscriptions & Billing](subscriptions-and-billing.md) for how plans and trials work.
4. You're in — the person who creates the organisation becomes its administrator, with access to invite the rest of the team and configure settings.

Every future sign-in — for you and every product you use — goes through the same central login;
see [Signing In](organisation/signing-in.md) for what that screen looks like and the other ways
in (Google/Microsoft/GitHub, passkeys).

## Setting up branches

If your business operates from more than one location — a second shop, a central warehouse, a
different outlet type — add each one as a **branch** under **My Organization → Branches**. The
use case you pick per branch (retail, hospitality, pharmacy, and so on) decides which products and
item types that branch actually uses — see
[Branches](organisation/managing-your-organisation.md#branches) for the full walkthrough. A
single-location business can skip this entirely and keep using its one default branch.

## Adding your team

Once your organisation is set up, invite your staff under **My Organization → Team**:

1. **Email** — an existing account is added directly; a brand-new email gets an account created on
   the spot, with a temporary password shown to you once to share securely.
2. **Role** — cashier, manager, accountant, and so on, depending on the product. Each product has
   its own set of roles suited to how that part of the business runs (a POS cashier's permissions
   look different from an ERP payroll administrator's), but everyone logs in through the same
   account.
3. **PIN** (optional) — set it now or later; this is what staff type on a shared till or warehouse
   tablet's PIN login screen instead of a full email/password sign-in.
4. **Outlet** (optional) — ties the member to one branch, if you've set any up.

See [Managing Your Organisation → Team](organisation/managing-your-organisation.md#team) for the
full walkthrough, including changing a role or resetting a password later.

## Adding a product

Your organisation isn't locked into the products you picked at signup. As your business grows — or if you're expanding into a new part of the business — you can add another product (say, moving from just POS to also using Treasury for full accounting) without creating a new account or re-entering your team. See [Billing](organisation/managing-your-organisation.md#billing) to pick a plan for a new product.

## Getting help

Each product includes its own in-app guidance for day-to-day tasks — see the
[Service Guides](platform-overview.md#finding-a-guide-for-a-specific-product) for step-by-step,
screenshot-led walkthroughs of Inventory, Notifications, and Treasury. Beyond what's written down,
the chat icon on any page reaches **Vera**, the platform's AI assistant, which can escalate to
your account contact when it can't answer something itself.
