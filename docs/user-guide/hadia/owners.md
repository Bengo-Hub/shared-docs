# For hosts

## Create your account

Open `hadiafrica.com` and press **Create a registry**. Enter your mobile number, press **Send code**
and type the code from WhatsApp, or press **Continue with Google or email**. Add your name, confirm
you are 18 or over, accept the terms and press **Continue**.

## Create and publish a registry

1. Press **New registry**, pick the occasion and press **Next**.
2. Enter the **Registry name** and **Event date**. The location is optional and never appears in
   link previews.
3. Add a **Message to guests** if you like and press **Save and continue**. The registry is a draft
   that only you can see.
4. Add gifts and funds. Press **Preview as guest** to check them.
5. Press **Publish**. If you see **Publishing shortly**, keep going. It goes live by itself within a
   few minutes.

## Add gifts and cash funds

On the **Gifts and funds** tab:

- **Add a gift**: a name and, if you like, a price, **How many** and a **Store link**.
- **Add from a link**: paste a product link from any online shop and press **Read the page**. Check
  each field and tap **Looks right**, or edit it.
- **Add a cash fund**: for a honeymoon, school fees or any bigger plan. Set a **Target** or leave it
  open.

For each gift, choose **How guests can give it**: **Reserve and buy**, **Contribute money**, or
**Either**. With **Either**, a gift takes one way at a time. While it is reserved it takes no money,
and once money arrives it can no longer be reserved.

A priced gift stops taking money once fully funded. A cash fund keeps taking gifts after its target;
guests stop seeing the target, and you see **Target reached** with the amount over. Hadia tells you
as each fund passes half, three quarters and its target.

**Archive** hides a gift from guests and keeps its history. **Delete** works only on gifts nobody has
reserved or given toward.

On the **Details** tab, add a cover photo, choose **How long a reservation lasts** (7 or 14 days) and
decide whether to **Show guests who reserved each gift** by first name.

## Share and privacy

Press **Share** to send the link by **WhatsApp** or **SMS**, **Copy link**, or **Download QR code**
for cards and the programme. If the link reached the wrong people, press **Make a new link**; the old
link and QR code stop working at once.

Press **Who can see it** to choose:

- **Public**: anyone can find it, including search engines.
- **Anyone with the link**: the default.
- **Link and passcode**: guests also need a passcode of 6 to 32 letters or numbers. Send it with the
  link.
- **Only me**: nobody else can open it.

If the registry holds a child's name, photo or birth details, tick **This registry holds information
about a child**. It then cannot be public, and photos stay off the guest page until you record
consent as a parent or guardian.

## Gift log, thank-yous and statements

Open **Gift log** from the registry to see every gift, who sent it, what it was for and the message.
Choose **Not thanked**, press **Thank**, then **Open WhatsApp** to send a ready thank-you.

**Gifts bought for you** lists items guests marked as bought. Press **I received it** when each one
arrives.

The **Statement** lists every gift and payout. Press **View PDF**, **Download Excel** or **Download
CSV**.

To choose how you hear about gifts, go to **Account** and pick **Tell me about each gift** or **One
summary a day at 6 pm**.

## Payouts

### Confirm your payout number

1. Go to **Account**, **Payout number**, and press **Add a payout number**.
2. Enter an M-Pesa number and press **Send the code**.
3. Type the code and press **Confirm the number**.

The first payout to a new number waits 48 hours. This protects you if someone else changes your
number. The same wait applies after **Change the number**.

### Take a payout

1. Open **Payouts** on the registry.
2. Press **Request payout**. Leave the amount blank to take everything, or enter part of it.
3. Press **See what you receive** and check the commission and **You receive**.
4. Press **Request payout** and confirm.

Only one payout runs at a time. A first or large payout may show **Waiting for approval** while
Hadia's finance team checks it; the money stays in the registry meanwhile. If a payout went wrong,
press **Report a problem** beside it.

Switch on **Pay out automatically** to be paid by itself once the registry reaches an amount you
choose.

### What fees mean

Guests pay a handling fee for their payment and see it with the exact total before paying. Hadia also
takes a commission on money gifts. Under **Who pays Hadia's commission**, choose **Guests pay it**,
which new registries start with, or **It comes out of my payouts**. A change applies from the next
gift. Every payout shows the exact figures before you confirm.

## Close or reopen a registry

On the **Settings** tab, under **Close or cancel**:

- **Close registry** stops new gifts and reservations. Your money can still be paid out.
- **Reopen registry** opens it again, within 90 days of closing.
- **Cancel registry** refunds guests and cannot be undone. Handling fees are not refunded, nor is
  money already paid out to you. Follow each refund on **Refunds**.

A registry also closes by itself a set number of days after the event, as the **Automatic payouts**
section of the payouts page explains. What is left then pays out to you, even with automatic payouts
off. You can still reopen it within 90 days.

## Premium

On the **Settings** tab, press **See what premium adds to this registry**. Premium is one payment for
one registry and never renews. The price shows before you pay. It adds a custom link after
`hadiafrica.com/r/`, a printable gift list as a PDF or spreadsheet, and quicker payouts that do not
wait for the automatic payout amount.
