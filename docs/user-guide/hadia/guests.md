# For guests

You need no app and no account. Everything works from the registry link in your phone's browser.

## Open a registry

Tap the link the host sent you. Or open `hadiafrica.com`, press **Find a registry**, and type the
code from the link or paste the whole link. Public registries can also be found by the names on
them.

If the hosts set a passcode, type the one they shared with you and press **Open the registry**.

The registry shows the hosts' message, the **Gifts** and the **Cash funds**. Each gift shows whether
it is still available, already reserved, bought or fully funded.

## The one rule for each gift

A gift is either reserved by one guest to buy, or funded with money by guests together. Never both at
the same time.

- If nobody has given yet, you can press **Reserve** or **Give**, whichever the hosts allow.
- If guests are already chipping in, the gift can no longer be reserved. You can add money to it.
- If a guest has reserved it, it takes no money while the reservation lasts. If they let it go, it
  opens again.
- A gift that is bought or fully funded is closed. The page says why.

## Reserve a gift to buy

1. Press **Reserve** on the gift.
2. Enter your first name. The hosts see it. Other guests do not, unless the hosts show first names.
3. If you like, press **Add my phone number**, then **Send me a code** and type the code. We use the
   number only to remind you before the reservation ends.
4. Tick the box to agree to Hadia's terms and privacy notice, then press **Reserve this gift**.

Before you confirm, the page shows how long the gift is held for you and the date it ends. A hold
lasts the number of days the hosts chose, and it never runs past the event day. With a phone number
you get a reminder 2 days before the end and another on the last day. Without one, note the date.

When it is done, press **Copy my reservation link** and keep it. It is the only way to change your
reservation.

## Change or cancel your reservation

Open your reservation link. From there you can:

- **Mark as bought** once you have the gift. Adding a receipt photo is optional. The hosts see the
  gift is on its way.
- **Ask for more time**. You can extend once, never past the event day.
- **Cancel my reservation** if your plans change. You can add **A note to the hosts**, such as why
  plans changed. The hosts receive it by email, and the gift is free for other guests straight away.

## Give money

1. Press **Give** on a cash fund or a gift.
2. Enter an amount in whole shillings. The page shows the smallest and largest amount you can give.
3. Press **See the total**. Check **Your gift**, the **Handling fee** and **Total you pay**.
4. Enter the M-Pesa number that should get the payment prompt. The hosts never see the number.
5. Add your name and **A message for the hosts** if you like. Both are optional.
6. Tick the box to agree to Hadia's terms and privacy notice, then press **Pay** with the total
   shown on the button.

Money toward a gift goes to the hosts as money, even if the gift is never fully funded.

### Pay with the M-Pesa prompt

A prompt arrives on your phone. Enter your PIN on that prompt only. The page updates by itself when
the payment goes through.

If the prompt is declined, cancelled or times out, press **Send a new prompt**, or **Pay by paybill
instead**.

### Pay by paybill

Sometimes the prompt cannot reach your phone, or M-Pesa prompts are slow. The page then shows the
paybill details.

1. In M-Pesa, choose **Lipa na M-Pesa**, then **Pay Bill**.
2. Enter business number **756756**.
3. Enter the account number shown on the page. Each gift gets its own account number, so copy it
   exactly.
4. Enter the amount shown and your PIN.

Confirmation is usually quick. When M-Pesa is busy, it can take up to an hour. You can close the
page; your receipt comes to your phone.

**Never pay twice.** Pay one way only. If the prompt still arrives after you paid by paybill, ignore
it. If you reopen the page, it shows the same paybill details for that gift. Use those, and do not
start a new payment.

Only ever pay paybill 756756 with the account number shown on the registry page. Hadia never asks you
to pay any other number.

## Your receipt

When your gift is in, the page says **Thank you, your gift is in** and shows your **Receipt number**.
A receipt also comes to your phone, and the hosts are told.

## If something looks wrong

At the foot of every registry page, press **Report this registry**. Our team reviews every report,
and the hosts are not told who sent it. See also [Staying safe](safety.md).
