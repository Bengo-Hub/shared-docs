Platform licence

Shaba Village receives a non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to use the platform for the duration of the service agreement, included in the monthly fee. Shaba Village's own developers, or another vendor it appoints, may use the documented APIs with keys issued to Shaba Village. The licence passes to the management corporation, at Shaba's direction, on handover of the estate.
