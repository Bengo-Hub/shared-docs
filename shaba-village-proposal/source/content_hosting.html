Hosting, backup and disaster recovery

Where tenant data is held, how it is backed up and how service is restored after a failure. Hosting is included in the monthly fee.

Hosting outside Kenya is a cross-border transfer under sections 48 to 50 of the Data Protection Act. It is documented in the DPIA with its safeguards: provider security certification, encryption in transit and of backups, access restriction and a data processing agreement. Hosting in a Kenyan data centre is available on request and would be quoted as a change to the monthly fee.

Recovery objectives

ObjectiveTargetMeaning for tenants
Recovery point objective (RPO)24 hoursIn the worst case, total loss of the server, data is restored from the last nightly backup. Payments made since then are recovered from M-Pesa statements and re-matched, so no payment is lost. Meter readings, work order updates and gate logs from that window may need to be re-entered or re-synchronised from the gate tablets.
Recovery time objective (RTO)4 hoursService is restored on a replacement server within 4 hours of a declared disaster.
Optional enhanced RPO15 minutesContinuous database log archiving to offsite storage, quoted on request.

Restore testing

A restore drill is performed quarterly: the latest offsite backup is restored into an isolated environment, record counts and ledger balances are compared with production, and a short report is shared with tenants on request. The first drill takes place before launch, in week 13.

Platform outage response

ScenarioImpactResponse
Service or pod failureSeconds to minutesAutomatic restart; two replicas per service; health checks with alerting
Server failure or provider outagePortal unavailableRebuild on a replacement server from version-controlled configuration, restore of the latest backup and re-matching of M-Pesa payments
During any outageOwners cannot sign inPaybill payments still reach each tenant's account; any confirmation missed during the outage is recovered from the M-Pesa statement and matched on recovery. Gate tablets continue offline with cached passes. Affected tenants are notified within 30 minutes of a confirmed incident and updated every 2 hours.
