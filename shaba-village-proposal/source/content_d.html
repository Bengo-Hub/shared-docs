Unit resale or transfer

  1. The selling owner requests a clearance certificate.
  2. The platform checks that all estate balances are nil and issues the certificate with a reference and expiry. auto
  3. On the transfer date, the old ownership closes, its account is finalised, and the new owner is invited. Meter readings are taken on the day so water is split correctly.
  4. Service charge for the month is apportioned by days between the two owners.

Owner letting to a tenant

  1. The owner adds the tenant as an occupant with start and end dates.
  2. The owner chooses which charges the tenant receives (water, garbage, parking).
  3. The tenant signs in by phone, creates visitor passes, raises requests and pays assigned bills with the unit account number.
  4. On the end date, the tenant's access ends automatically. A final meter reading is requested. auto

Notices and alerts

  1. The estate manager writes a notice, selects the audience (estate, block, owners, occupants) and channels.
  2. Emergency alerts are sent immediately on all channels. Routine notices respect quiet hours, 21:00 to 07:00.
  3. Delivery status per recipient is shown; failed SMS falls back to WhatsApp where available.

Annual budget and AGM

  1. Finance prepares the next year's budget by cost line in treasury, with the prior year's actuals beside it.
  2. The proposed service charge per unit type is calculated from the budget and unit entitlements.
  3. The AGM pack (notice, budget, income and expenditure statement, sinking fund statement) is published in the document library and notified to owners.
  4. Rates approved at the AGM are entered with their effective date and apply from the next billing run.

Handover to the management corporation

  1. The estate fund ledger, unit accounts, sinking fund balance, vendor contracts, asset register and documents are exported.
  2. The corporation is set up as its own tenant, or Shaba Village continues as managing agent under a new mandate.
  3. Paybills and bank accounts are transferred or mandates changed, and opening balances are carried over and reconciled.

Data subject requests

  1. An owner, occupant, visitor or agency worker submits a request for access, correction or deletion.
  2. Identity is confirmed by one-time password and the statutory deadline is recorded.
  3. Access requests produce a machine-readable export.
  4. Deletion erases or anonymises personal data. Financial, sale and tax records required by law are retained for the statutory period, and the requester is told what is kept and why.

ThreatControls
Account takeoverOTP rate limits by number and IP, hashed codes, short expiry, session revocation, MFA for staff, alerts on new device sign-in for finance roles
One resident seeing another's dataEvery request checked against the caller's unit links on the server; IDs alone never grant access; tests for each role

Codevertex access to estate data

Codevertex staff do not access any tenant's personal or financial records in normal operation. Access occurs only to resolve a support request, investigate an incident or perform a restore, is limited to the records concerned, and is logged. These obligations are set out in the data processing agreement, and every engineer with production access signs a confidentiality undertaking.

Penetration testing

An internal review against the OWASP Application Security Verification Standard is included in week 13. An independent penetration test is recommended before launch and annually, commissioned at the launch tenant's option and cost; Codevertex remediates all high and critical findings at no charge before launch.
