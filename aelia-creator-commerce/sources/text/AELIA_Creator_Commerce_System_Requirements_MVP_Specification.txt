PART 1 — PLATFORM OVERVIEW 
1.1 Purpose 
AELIA Creator Commerce is a technology-enabled marketplace connecting brands with creators for influence and content, while 
providing the tools required to match, collaborate, contract, pay, measure and build repeat commercial relationships. 
Core Commercial Journey 
Marketing Objective → Creator Match → Campaign → Content → Commerce → Measurement → Relationship  
1.2 Platform Model 
Component Requirement 
Influence Marketplace Creators publish content to their own audiences on behalf of brands.  
Content Marketplace Creators produce content for brands, whether or not they publish it to their own audiences.  
Brand Side Brands discover creators, create campaigns, collaborate, manage content, fund campaigns and measure results.  
Creator Side Creators build professional profiles, discover opportunities, deliver campaigns, get paid and build commercial relationships.  
AELIA Operations Verification, matching support, campaign administration, payment monitoring, disputes, reporting and platform management.

1.4 Business Objectives 
# Objective 
1 Connect brands with relevant creators. 
2 Turn marketing objectives into structured creator campaigns.  
3 Create professional commercial opportunities for creators.  
4 Facilitate campaign collaboration and payment.  
5 Measure campaign and commercial outcomes. 
6 Build two-sided reputation and trust. 
7 Enable repeat bookings and relationships. 
8 Build creator-commerce data and intelligence. 
9 Generate sustainable platform revenue. 
PART 2 — CORE FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS 
Platform Module What the System Must Do 
User Registration & Verification Register and verify Brands, Creators and authorized platform users. 
Creator Passport Capture creator identity, niche, skills, platforms, audience, performance, rates, packages, availability, usage rights and ca mpaign history. 
Brand Passport Capture organization information, industry, location, verification, campaign history, payment reliability and commercial repu tation. 
Creator Discovery Allow brands to search, filter, review and shortlist suitable creators. 
Creator Matching Recommend creators based on audience, niche, platform, geography, performance, budget, reliability, suitability and availabil ity. 
Campaign Creation Allow brands to define objective, audience, platforms, deliverables, creators, budget, timeline, content requirements and rig hts. 
Campaign Workspace Manage briefs, creators, agreements, messages, deliverables, deadlines, content, approvals, payments and performance in one p lace. 
Content Marketplace Support both influence campaigns and UGC/content-production campaigns. 
Content Submission Allow creators to submit content, receive revisions and obtain approval. 
Contracts & Rights Record deliverables, fees, deadlines, revisions, cancellation, ownership, usage, geography, exclusivity and other agreed term s. 
Communication Enable campaign-related messaging and notifications. 
Payments Facilitate campaign funding, transaction tracking, creator payouts and reconciliation through an appropriate payment provider . 
Performance Tracking Track reach, views, engagement, clicks, leads, conversions and sales where technically measurable.  
Reputation Enable Brands to review Creators and Creators to review Brands. 
Rebooking Enable repeat bookings, saved creators, future invitations and ongoing relationships. 
Reporting Provide campaign, financial and performance reporting. 
Administration Manage verification, campaigns, transactions, disputes, users, analytics and platform configuration.

PART 4 — TECHNICAL, DATA & TRUST REQUIREMENTS 
Area Requirement 
Architecture AELIA should own its application and platform data while integrating approved Codevertex shared services.  
Frontend Responsive web / PWA for MVP; architecture should remain mobile -ready. 
Backend Agreed AELIA / Codevertex architecture with documented services and responsibilities.  
Database AELIA-specific database and data model. 
Authentication Secure authentication and role-based access. 
APIs Complete documentation for all shared services and external integrations.  
Payments Use an appropriate licensed payment provider; AELIA should not assume it operates regulated payment/escrow services.  
Social Integrations Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn or other platforms where APIs and permissions permit.  
Security Authentication, authorization, encryption, API security, audit logs, backups and monitoring.  
Privacy Consent, privacy information, retention, deletion, user access, processor management and breach response.  
Data Model Users, organizations, creators, brands, campaigns, deliverables, agreements, transactions, payouts, reviews, disputes and per formance. 
Notifications Email, SMS, WhatsApp where available and in -platform notifications. 
Analytics Campaign, creator, brand, financial and platform performance data.

CORE MVP PRINCIPLE 
Connect a real brand with a suitable creator, manage the campaign, facilitate payment, measure the outcome 
and create the basis for repeat business. 
The MVP should therefore prioritize commercial validation, usability, trust, transaction integrity, campaign delivery, measurement and 
repeat business rather than attempting to deliver every planned future capability.
