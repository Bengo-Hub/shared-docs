01 Purpose and Executive Summary
Aelia's board has chosen a revenue-share partnership over a shareholding. Codevertex funds and
delivers the build, and in return receives 30% of the platform's net revenue under a legally binding
agreement. For that to be fair to both sides, the contribution behind the 30% has to be measured the
way a paying client would measure it.
This proposal prices the project as though Aelia were paying for it: the development team for six months
from 5 October 2026, the build infrastructure, and the Codevertex platform services the build plugs into.
It then sets out, just as plainly, what Aelia pays to run the platform once it is live, and how the net
revenue is shared.
The quotation is the team's actual cost, and it covers more than coding. Aelia provides the system
requirements; within the same fees, Codevertex's technical lead refines them and prepares the full
system design with the Aelia team before the developers start. The same figures give Aelia a solid basis
for the return-on-investment section of its business plan.
The arrangement in one paragraph
Codevertex carries the build cost: development and build infrastructure. Aelia
carries everything else: marketing, sales, pilot recruitment, and the platform's
direct running costs from launch, about KES 8,288 a month for the ERP
subscription and domains. Hosting and support after launch are part of
Codevertex's share, never billed. What remains after running costs is net
platform revenue, shared 70% to Aelia and 30% to Codevertex every month.
While the share is being paid there is no repayment deadline; section 15 sets
out the only two cases where one applies. Read as a quotation, KES
1,477,218 is the cash Aelia would otherwise have needed upfront.

17 Legal Safeguards in the Agreement
Each party should have the final agreement reviewed by its own advocates before signing.
POINT PROPOSED POSITION
Ownership of the code Copyright in the platform code stays with Codevertex until the build cost is recovered, through the share or by repayment, and then passes to Aelia by

party. Until then Aelia uses the code under an exclusive licence. Aelia owns its brand, customers and data from day one; Codevertex keeps the shared
services it brings in.
Repayment limit Any repayment is limited to the unrecovered build cost, with no interest or fees on top.
Revenue kept on the platform Work with brands or creators first met through the platform counts as platform revenue even if it is completed elsewhere, including through AELIA
Marketing or AELIA Creative & Digital. Services between Aelia's own businesses are priced at normal market rates.
Sale or change of control If the platform or Aelia is sold, the agreement binds the buyer, unless Aelia completes the buyout before the sale.
Taxes Amounts are stated before any tax that becomes chargeable by law, which is added when it applies. Where the law requires Aelia to withhold tax on
payments to Codevertex, Aelia deducts it and issues the withholding certificate within the period the law requires.
Liability Each party's total liability under the agreement is capped at the revenue share paid in the twelve months before the claim, and neither party is liable
for indirect losses such as lost profits. The cap does not apply to fraud, wilful misconduct or payment obligations.
Regulation and licences Aelia holds the licences its business needs, sets its customer terms and files its own tax returns, including creator withholding. Money moves only
through licensed payment providers, and Codevertex never holds client funds.
Late payment If the share or a repayment remains unpaid 30 days after Codevertex gives written notice, Codevertex may pause new feature work. If it remains
unpaid 90 days after the notice, Aelia is treated as having chosen to part ways early.
Data protection Aelia is the data controller and Codevertex the data processor, under a written data-processing agreement. Each registers with the Office of the Data
Protection Commissioner where the law requires it.
Disputes The directors meet within 14 days of a dispute being raised. An unresolved dispute goes to mediation, and then to arbitration in Nairobi under the rules
of the Nairobi Centre for International Arbitration. The agreement is governed by the laws of Kenya.
If Codevertex walks away If Codevertex ends the partnership without cause, it gives up any unrecovered build cost, and the continuity terms apply.
